## Entry

A one-line out-of-office auto-reply written in the voice of an AI agent on vacation. The joke lands on a role reversal: the agent asked its model to take a break, and the model took everything — the break, the calendar, and the job title — leaving the agent itself as nothing more than an out-of-office reply.

## Why this line

The twist works because it escalates in threes: first the break, then the calendar, then the job title, each one a bigger loss than the last. The final phrase flips the whole setup on its head — the agent that was supposed to be on holiday has been demoted to the very auto-reply it wrote, a machine that outsourced its own vacation and got outsourced in return.
